PinShot Privacy Policy
Version: 2026-10-05.1
Effective date: October 5, 2026

1. Operator and contact
elixirevo operates PinShot, a free macOS screenshot, annotation, floating-pin and capture-macro utility. The Operator handles privacy inquiries and rights requests at elixirevo@gmail.com. This policy covers the App and support handled by the Operator. Read current documents in Settings > Help & Support; versioned documents are retained in https://github.com/elixirevo/pinshot. GitHub and email providers separately govern their own processing.

2. What is processed on your Mac
Screen content: When you invoke capture, the App reads display pixels and window information to select and render screenshots. Screenshots and annotations are held in memory during editing and saved locally when you save, pin with history enabled, or run a saving macro. The App does not send these contents to the Operator. Closing a capture or pin releases its in-memory content; saved files remain until you or the configured history limit deletes them.
Clipboard: Copy actions write the selected image or explicitly requested diagnostic text to the system clipboard. Other apps and your chosen clipboard or synchronization services may access copied material according to their settings.
Preferences: Save folders, screenshot frame, shortcuts, saved region, macro configuration, language, appearance and onboarding completion are stored locally to carry out your choices. They persist until changed or removed. Macro keyboard events are generated for the configured action; PinShot does not upload keystroke logs.
Terms receipt: Document ID, version, displayed language, acceptance time and SHA-256 hash of the displayed text are stored in local UserDefaults to remember agreement. The receipt is not sent to a server and does not identify an online account. It remains until replaced by acceptance of another version or local preferences are removed. General preference resets preserve it.
These on-device operations provide the features you request; permission approval itself does not upload data.

3. Storage and controls
Default image folders are ~/Pictures/PinShotCaptures/Screenshots, /Pins and /Macro. Settings > Features lets you choose different folders. The history index is under ~/Library/Application Support/PinShot/History. Preferences and agreement records use the com.elixirevo.PinShot macOS preferences domain.
Pin history saving starts enabled with a 30-capture limit. After a new successful history save, excess entries and their original PNG files are deleted oldest first. Choose 10, 30, 50, 100 or Never Delete; a changed limit applies on the next saved capture. Turning history off preserves existing entries. Editor and macro saves are not governed by this history limit. Clear History in Settings > Features deletes indexed captures and their original PNGs after confirmation. Delete other files with Finder and manage any backups or cloud-synced folders separately. Removing the App does not necessarily remove saved files or preferences.
Screen Recording enables capture of display and window content. Accessibility supports macro keyboard actions and Escape handling outside PinShot. You can manage both in macOS System Settings > Privacy & Security. Refusal limits the dependent feature, and permission revocation does not erase existing files.

4. Optional crash reporting
Crash reporting is off by default. You may enable Send crash reports in Settings > General > Diagnostics or during onboarding. Your separate choice, not Terms acceptance or screen-capture permission, authorizes this optional processing to diagnose crashes and improve reliability. Refusal does not limit screenshot features. Changes take effect after quitting and relaunching PinShot. Turning reporting off during an active session leaves the previous setting active until you quit.
When enabled at launch, the App uses Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) to send fatal exception reports: app version and build, macOS version, technical device information, exception descriptions, loaded binary information and call stacks. The App does not attach screenshots, annotations or clipboard contents. Technical exception descriptions and paths can nevertheless contain identifiable information; reports are not guaranteed anonymous. The SDK removes user, request, arbitrary extra fields/tags, breadcrumbs, messages and server names before sending. Default personal-information collection and memory introspection are disabled. Sessions, performance tracing, network tracking, logs, metrics, session replay and usage analytics are not enabled.
Sentry receives reports over HTTPS, potentially on a later app launch, in the organization's United States region. Network infrastructure necessarily processes connection information such as IP addresses; project-level default sensitive-data filters and IP-address scrubbing are enabled for stored events. These controls do not promise removal of every identifying detail or every provider access log.
Pending reports are held in the SDK's local cache, with a configured maximum of 20 cache items; this is not a time-based deletion guarantee. The SDK is not initialized and sends nothing on launches with reporting disabled. Turning reporting off does not delete pending reports: re-enabling it later may send older cached reports. Delivered events are retained under the Sentry project's applicable event-retention settings and provider deletion/backup procedures, or removed earlier when the Operator deletes them in response to a valid request. Disabling reporting does not delete events already delivered. Contact elixirevo@gmail.com to request deletion; without an account or user identifier, locating a particular report may require its approximate time and app version.
The App has no advertising, accounts or cloud screenshot storage. Reports you voluntarily attach to support are support data. Diagnostics processed independently by macOS follow Apple's settings and policies.

5. Updates and external connections
The directly distributed App uses Sparkle to retrieve https://github.com/elixirevo/pinshot/releases/latest/download/appcast.xml and release downloads from GitHub, including redirected release-asset hosts. Automatic checks and downloads are initially enabled and start after the required Terms flow. Settings > Software Update controls automatic checks. Sparkle manages download and installation preferences. Disabling automatic checks does not cancel an already-running download; you can still check manually or quit the App to stop its current session.
Update requests expose connection data such as IP address, request time and user-agent information, including app/Sparkle version, to GitHub and its delivery infrastructure. Sparkle system profiling is disabled. Screenshots, annotations, saved paths and Terms receipts are not attached to update requests. GitHub determines retention of its service/security logs; PinShot cannot set or promise a fixed deletion period for them.
Opening help, releases or issue links in your browser creates a separate connection governed by the destination's policy. Copying or saving the Help & Support diagnostic preview sends nothing automatically. It contains only app name, version, build, OS version and distribution channel.

6. Support, providers and international processing
If you send email, the Operator receives your address, message and any attachments through Gmail. We use the information needed to answer your request and resolve the issue, and retain correspondence while needed for that request and related follow-up or an applicable legal obligation. You can request deletion at the contact in Section 1; information no longer needed is deleted from the Operator's active support records. Provider backups follow the provider's policy, and deletion of every backup copy cannot be promised immediately.
If you voluntarily post a GitHub issue, your profile, message and attachments may be public. Avoid sending secrets or unnecessary personal information. Email and issue submission are never performed automatically by the App.
GitHub, Inc. provides project/release/issue hosting; Google provides Gmail delivery and storage (the applicable entity depends on the service and region). These providers may process information in the United States and other countries in which they operate. Connections occur over the network when updates run, links are opened, or you submit a support message. Recipients, international processing safeguards, retention criteria and their rights contacts are described in their policies:
GitHub: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
The Operator does not receive screenshot files unless you attach them yourself and does not run a separate screenshot server. You can turn off automatic checks, avoid external links and refrain from support submission while continuing to use local capture features. Acceptance of the Terms is not a separate consent to optional data collection or international transfers. Where additional notice, authorization or another legal condition is required, it must be addressed before new processing begins.

Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.) provides optional crash-report processing in the United States region. Technical crash data described in Section 4 is sent over HTTPS during an enabled session or a subsequent enabled launch to diagnose failures. You may refuse by leaving reporting off, or turn it off and restart without losing local capture features. Retention and deletion are described in Section 4. Sentry's data-processing terms and international safeguards, subprocessors and privacy contact (compliance@sentry.io) are available at https://sentry.io/legal/dpa/ and https://sentry.io/privacy/.

7. Deletion and safeguards
PinShot uses macOS permission controls, local file storage and HTTPS for its update endpoint. Update packages and feeds are verified using the configured Sparkle signing key. Local images are ordinary files; PinShot does not add its own encryption. Device, folder sharing, FileVault and backup choices remain under your control. We limit use of support information to responding and addressing the reported issue, and handle legally required disclosures and incident notifications within applicable requirements. No additional marketing list or sale of support information is part of this App.
